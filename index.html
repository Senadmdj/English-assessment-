A CITY THAT REWARDS CURIOSITY

LET THE
DESTINATION
COME ALIVE.

Yangzhou is a historic city in Jiangsu, China, known for its waterways, classical gardens and cultural heritage. Along Slender West Lake, willow-lined banks, elegant bridges and pavilions create a calm setting for a slow walk or boat ride.

Traditional gardens invite visitors to notice carefully framed views, stonework, plants and the balance between architecture and nature. The city's food culture offers another way to explore its character, with local flavours best discovered through trusted restaurants and neighbourhood businesses. From the Grand Canal's long history to the quieter corners of the old town, Yangzhou rewards travellers who take time to look closely and explore respectfully.

Paradise View is your starting point for discovering the landscapes, experiences and local character that make a destination memorable. The campaign brings scenic inspiration together with cultural discovery and local enterprise, encouraging visitors to travel thoughtfully and connect with the people and places that shape Yangzhou.

A DESTINATION FOR CURIOUS MINDS

COME AS YOU ARE.
LEAVE WITH A STORY.

Whether travelling from elsewhere in China or arriving from overseas, visitors can discover Yangzhou through its landscapes, gardens, food culture and heritage. Paradise View speaks to culture lovers, families, couples, photographers and travellers who value meaningful experiences.

As a destination-level campaign, its purpose is not to promote one hotel or tour operator. It brings attention to the wider destination and encourages visitors to explore respectfully, check reliable local information and support the restaurants, cultural attractions and small businesses that contribute to local life.
